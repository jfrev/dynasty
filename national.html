Week 10

Slammin Beers Resigns as Arizona State Head Coach, Enters Alcohol Treatment Program

Beers steps down following Arizona State's loss to Utah; the school confirmed an indefinite suspension alongside news that he has voluntarily entered a treatment facility.

By the Dispatch Editorial Board · National Desk

Arizona State head coach Slammin Beers has resigned, the school confirmed this week, days after a lopsided home loss to No. 6 Utah. In a joint statement, Beers and the university said he has voluntarily checked into an alcohol treatment facility and will be stepping away from coaching indefinitely to focus on his health.

Beers took over the Sun Devils program this season as a first-time head coach and led the team to a 6-1 start before back-to-back losses closed out his tenure. Players and staff who worked under him this season described a coach who built real early results, even as his personal circumstances became a larger story than anyone anticipated back in August.

Arizona State has not named a permanent replacement as of publication. The Dispatch wishes Beers well in his recovery and will share more on the program's next steps as they develop.

Week 10

First College Football Playoff Bracket Revealed — Kentucky Projects as a Top Seed

The season's first playoff projection is out, and it's a strong one for the Big 12's newest contender.

By the Dispatch Editorial Board · National Desk

The initial College Football Playoff bracket dropped this week, and undefeated No. 3 Kentucky projects as one of the top seeds in the field after a 9-0 start under first-year coach LaSperm — the highest the committee has ever ranked a Kentucky team in program history.

Elsewhere in the projected field, both Army (No. 10) and Boston State (No. 11) currently sit inside the bracket picture, while Rhode Island (No. 20) is on the outside looking in for now. With half the schedule still to play, plenty of movement remains possible before the field is finalized.

The committee's full rationale and complete national bracket are available on the CFP's official site. The Dispatch will track how our programs' postseason chances shift with every update.
