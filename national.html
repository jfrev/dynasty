Slammin Waters Named Interim Head Coach at Arizona State

The program moves forward following Slammin Beers' resignation, tapping a familiar-sounding name to steady the ship.

By the Dispatch Editorial Board · National Desk

Arizona State has named Slammin Waters as interim head coach, the school confirmed this week, filling the vacancy left by Slammin Beers' resignation and entry into an alcohol treatment program. Waters, an existing member of the on-campus staff, will lead the program through the remainder of the season.

The school reiterated its support for Beers' recovery and said no timeline has been set for a permanent hiring search. For now, Arizona State's focus shifts to steadying a season that started 6-1 before Beers' departure derailed it.

Week 11

Spotted: Rhode Island's Tanka Jahari Ordering an Entire Pizza to Himself

A harmless local sighting has become the fanbase's favorite running joke about their first-year coach.

By the Dispatch Editorial Board · National Desk

Not every national item has to be a scandal. Rhode Island head coach Tanka Jahari was spotted at a restaurant near campus this week ordering, and finishing, an entire pizza by himself — a perfectly normal thing to do that has nonetheless become a minor sensation among Ghost Bat fans, who've adopted it as an endearing bit of program lore during the program's best season in years.
