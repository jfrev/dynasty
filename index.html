Week 10 · National News

Slammin Beers Resigns as Arizona State Head Coach, Enters Alcohol Treatment Program

Beers steps down following Arizona State's loss to Utah; the school confirmed an indefinite suspension alongside news that he has voluntarily entered a treatment facility.

By the Dispatch Editorial Board · Week 10

Arizona State head coach Slammin Beers has resigned, the school confirmed this week, days after a lopsided home loss to No. 6 Utah. In a joint statement, Beers and the university said he has voluntarily checked into an alcohol treatment facility and will be stepping away from coaching indefinitely to focus on his health.

Beers took over the Sun Devils program this season as a first-time head coach and led the team to a 6-1 start before back-to-back losses closed out his tenure. Players and staff who worked under him this season described a coach who built real early results, even as his personal circumstances became a larger story than anyone anticipated back in August.

Arizona State has not named a permanent replacement as of publication. The Dispatch wishes Beers well in his recovery and will share more on the program's next steps as they develop, on our National News desk.

Week 10 · Game of the Week

Florida State Visits No. 23 Wisconsin as Both Programs Ride Winning Streaks Into a Ranked Showdown

Army already took care of Air Force 14-0. This week's marquee matchup is a fresh one: two teams playing their best football of the season collide in Madison.

By the Dispatch Editorial Board · Week 10

Wisconsin cracked the rankings for the first time all season on the strength of back-to-back wins over Miami and Pitt, checking in at No. 23 just as Florida State — a winner of two straight of its own, including a 31-24 road win at Boston College — comes to Camp Randall. Both programs are playing their best football of the year at the same time, which makes this an easy call for the week's biggest game.

Wisconsin's identity hasn't changed all season: win the line of scrimmage, run the ball until the defense caves, make the other team play from behind. Florida State counters with a talented, veteran roster that's finally putting it together under Guardiola, even with two coordinators already told they won't be back next year.

Home field and a more physical identity give Wisconsin a slight edge in the Dispatch's model, but not much of one — this is as even a matchup as the league has produced all season.

Week 10 · Recap

No. 9 Army Blanks Air Force 14-0 to Extend Its Winning Streak to Three

Last week's game of the week lived up to the rivalry billing. Army heads to TCU next.

By the Dispatch Editorial Board · Week 10

Army took care of business against Air Force, 14-0, in a game that played out exactly like the low-scoring, option-heavy rivalry battle everyone expected. The Black Knights move to 7-1, 4-1 in the Big 12, climb to No. 9 nationally, and travel to TCU next still riding a three-game winning streak.
