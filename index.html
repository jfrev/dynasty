Week 9 · Game of the Week

Clemson Visits Tallahassee With Florida State's Season — and Its Coach's Week — on the Line

No game on this week's slate carries more weight than Clemson at Florida State. The Dispatch breaks down the line.

By the Dispatch Editorial Board · Week 9

Every other storyline this week is a subplot compared to what's happening in Tallahassee. Florida State hosts Clemson at 4-3, 2-2 in the ACC, with a head coach fielding more questions about a viral sideline moment than his defensive scheme. A loss here and the phrase "under fire" stops being figurative.

Clemson arrives as the more battle-tested, more consistent team on paper, and the Dispatch model has them favored — but not by much. Florida State's talent level has never been the question this season; execution and, this week, off-field noise have been. Home crowds have a way of making that noise useful instead of distracting, and Guardiola's group has a real chance to flip the week's entire narrative with one good Saturday.

Both fan bases are locked in. Both coaching staffs know exactly what a result here does to the rest of the season's tone. This is as close to a must-win as either program has faced all year.

Week 9 · Recap

No. 5 Kentucky Demolishes Arizona State, 28-3, Vaults Into the Top Five

The Wildcats' toughest test of the season turned into their most lopsided win. Kentucky is 8-0 and, for the first time in decades, a top-five team.

By the Dispatch Editorial Board · Week 9

So much for a coin-flip game. Kentucky went into Tempe and left with a 28-3 win over Arizona State that was never as close as the pregame hype suggested. LaSperm's trenches-first identity smothered a Sun Devils offense that had scored freely all season, and Kentucky moves to 8-0, 5-0 in the Big 12, vaulting to No. 5 nationally.

Arizona State, now 6-2 and coming off its worst loss of the year under first-year coach Slammin Beers, gets almost no time to regroup — a home date with No. 6 Utah looms this very week.
