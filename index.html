Week 11 · Game of the Week

No. 7 Boston State Travels to No. 20 Wisconsin in the Biggest Game of the Season

Two of the league's hottest teams collide in Madison. The Dispatch has two different sportsbooks on the line this week.

By the Dispatch Editorial Board · Week 11

Boston State is 10-0 and hasn't been seriously tested since the Guardiola-era loss to nobody in particular — this program simply hasn't lost. Wisconsin enters on a three-game heater of its own, ranked for the first time all season after handling Florida State at home. Camp Randall gets the biggest crowd of the year for it.

Boston State's talent and depth are the best in the conference not named Kentucky. Wisconsin's identity — win the trenches, run the ball, make the other team play from behind — is exactly the style that gives high-powered offenses trouble on the road. This is as close to an even game as the Dispatch has graded all season.

With a game this big, we're running two lines this week from two different books — they don't agree on much, which tells you everything about how close this one really is.

Week 11 · Around the League

Perfect Records, a Coordinator Walkback, and an NIL Headache: A Loaded Week 11

Army and Rhode Island keep winning, Kentucky's bye week came with bad financial news, and Florida State's coaching staff drama took a strange turn.

By the Dispatch Editorial Board · Week 11

No. 9 Army survived its toughest test yet, holding off TCU 24-21 to push its winning streak to four and set up a road trip to Houston. No. 14 Rhode Island kept rolling too, routing Baylor 42-25 to crack its highest ranking of the season ahead of a trip to Oklahoma.

Kentucky's bye week was quiet on the field and loud off it — reports of a real NIL funding deficit are emerging in the wake of LaSperm's early exit from a booster event two weeks ago. And in Tallahassee, Guardiola walked back his own statement that Florida State's coordinators would be fired, while confirming the program will hit the transfer portal for a quarterback this offseason.
