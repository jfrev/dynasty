No. 5 Kentucky Rolls Past Arizona State, 28-3, Takes Perfect Record to West Virginia

The toughest test of the season turned into the least dramatic result of it. Kentucky is 8-0, No. 5 in the country, and still finding a higher gear.

By Callie Broussard · The Bluegrass Blitz

So much for a coin-flip game. Kentucky went into Tempe and left with a 28-3 win over Arizona State that was never as close as the pregame hype suggested, LaSperm's trenches-first identity smothering a Sun Devils offense that had scored freely all season. Kentucky moves to 8-0, 5-0 in the Big 12, and vaults to No. 5 nationally — the program's best ranking in decades.

If Odysseus needed a run-in with a Cyclops to prove his crew could handle anything, Kentucky just got its own monster-clearing performance out of the way early. The next stop is West Virginia, a step down in name value but the kind of trap game a perfect season lives to find out about.

Recruiting held steady with an addition: three-star safety Donald Daniel joined the 2027 class this week, which now sits at No. 9 nationally. Everything about this program right now is trending toward the kind of season nobody in Lexington wants to jinx by saying out loud.
