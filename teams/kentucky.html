Kentucky's Historic Season Ends in the Sugar Bowl: Boston State 28, Kentucky 14

A perfect regular season and a first-ever Big 12 title couldn't survive a national semifinal against the eventual champion.

By Callie Broussard · The Bluegrass Blitz

Kentucky's magical season came to an end in the Sugar Bowl, falling 28-14 to Boston State after advancing past Alabama in the Fiesta Bowl. The Wildcats close the year 13-1, 8-0 in the Big 12, with a program-first conference championship and a first-ever playoff appearance to show for it — even if the ending wasn't the one this locker room wanted.

LaSperm's first season delivered almost everything a program could ask for: a perfect regular season, a Big 12 title, a top-three ranking, and a genuine run at a national championship. Boston State simply proved to be the better team on the night, closing out its own perfect season a round later against Oregon.

Recruiting sits at No. 11 nationally heading into an offseason that will also include rebuilding the scouting department from scratch after this week's front-office shakeup. By any measure other than the final score of one game, this was the best season in Kentucky football history.
