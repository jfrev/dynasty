No. 3 Kentucky Enjoys Its Bye, But an NIL Deficit Is Becoming a Real Problem

The perfect record hasn't slowed down. The booster relationships backing it up might have.

By Callie Broussard · The Bluegrass Blitz

Kentucky didn't play last week, and the record stayed put at 10-0, 6-0 in the Big 12, with the program settling in at No. 3 nationally. The Wildcats host Oklahoma State this week looking to keep the perfect season alive.

The bigger story has been building quietly since LaSperm's early exit from that booster event: the program is now reportedly facing a real NIL funding deficit, with several major donors said to be less engaged since the incident. It's not yet affecting the roster on the field, but staff have acknowledged internally that the pipeline that funded this year's success is not as reliable as it was in August.

Recruiting held at No. 11 nationally. For a program riding the best season in its history, the growing distance between LaSperm and the boosters who help pay for it all is the one loose thread nobody in Lexington has a clean answer for yet.
