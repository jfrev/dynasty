Week 10

No. 18 Rhode Island Rolls Oklahoma State 41-13, Travels to Baylor Next

A lopsided win backed up the ranking. Now the recruiting class jumping to No. 8 nationally backs up the whole season.

By Tomas Reyes · The Ocean State Outlook

Rhode Island answered any doubts about its ranking with its most complete performance of the season, routing Oklahoma State 41-13 to move to 6-3 and climb to No. 18 nationally. The Ghost Bats travel to Baylor next, looking to keep building a season that's already exceeded every preseason expectation.

The bigger news arrived on the recruiting trail: five new commitments — four-star defensive tackle Greg Keur, four-star guard Fredrick Coats, four-star edge Chauncey Purvis, four-star linebacker Jose Newcomb, and four-star running back Jimmy Chamblin — rocketed the 2027 class from No. 35 to No. 8 nationally, by far the biggest jump of any program in the dynasty this year.

Nobody in the building is getting ahead of themselves — Jahari's message hasn't changed since August — but between the ranking, the blowout win, and the recruiting class, Rhode Island's rebuild is starting to look less like a one-year story and more like a genuine foundation.

Week 11

No. 14 Rhode Island Routs Baylor 42-25, Travels to Oklahoma Next

Another lopsided win pushed Rhode Island into the top 15. The Ghost Bats hit the road again this week.

By Tomas Reyes · The Ocean State Outlook

Rhode Island kept rolling, putting up 42 points on Baylor in a 42-25 win that pushed the Ghost Bats to 7-3, 3-3 in the Big 12, and into the rankings at No. 14 — a new high for the program. Oklahoma is next on the road, another chance to prove this season is built on more than one good stretch.

Coach Tanka Jahari has stayed exactly as understated as ever off the field, drawing a bit of local attention this week for reportedly ordering an entire pizza to himself at a spot near campus — the kind of harmless, distinctly Jahari detail that's become its own small running joke among the fanbase.

Recruiting held at No. 8 nationally, still the biggest riser of any program in the dynasty this season. Between the ranking, the win total, and the recruiting class, Rhode Island's turnaround keeps finding new gears.
