Preseason

Nobody's Talking About the Sun Devils. Their Brand-New Head Coach Would Prefer It Stay That Way

Slammin Beers gets his first head coaching job in the desert, inheriting a roster that's better than its reputation.

By Nico Alvarado · The Desert Fork Report

Preseason polls are a popularity contest as much as anything else, and Arizona State has never been great at winning that part of the sport. This offseason adds a new twist: first-time head coach Slammin Beers.

Whatever else Beers is, he's not shy — camp reports describe a program that runs loud, physical practices.

If there's a knock, it's that nobody has any real data on how Beers manages a season yet. We're finding out fast.

Unbeaten Meets Undefeated: No. 11 Kentucky Comes to Tempe, and a Top-5 Class Arrives With It

Arizona State is 6-1, hosting an undefeated Kentucky team, and quietly building the No. 5 recruiting class in the country.

By Nico Alvarado · The Desert Fork Report

Slammin Beers wanted this game the moment the schedule came out. Hosting an undefeated, No. 11 Kentucky team in Tempe is about as loud a stage as this program gets this year.

Vegas and the model both lean Kentucky slightly, but this Arizona State team has made a habit of playing its best football at home.
