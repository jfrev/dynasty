Preseason

Guardiola Comes Home — And Brings Championship Expectations With Him

The Seminoles didn't just keep their talent this offseason. They got their old coach back, fresh off a title at USC.

By Dana Marchetti · The Renegade Report

Fall camp closed the way it usually does at Florida State: with more questions about who plays too many positions well than who doesn't play any well enough. This year there's a new wrinkle — the man running the whole operation is Pipo Guardiola, back in Tallahassee for the first time since he left for USC, a national championship ring now in his pocket.

The homecoming storyline writes itself, but the roster underneath it is what actually has this building buzzing.

Nobody in this building is talking about just making a bowl game. The bar is the same as it's always been in Tallahassee.

Week 9

Guardiola Under Fire After Mocking a Fan's Hairline During the Week 6 Loss to Boston State

Sideline video from Florida State's Week 6 defeat to Boston State resurfaced this week, and it has not gone well for the head coach.

By Dana Marchetti · The Renegade Report

It took three weeks for it to surface, but a clip from Florida State's Week 6 home loss to Boston State is now the biggest story surrounding the program — not the 2-2 conference record. Sideline video shows Pipo Guardiola, mid-timeout, gesturing toward a heckling fan and mouthing something about the man's hairline before turning back to his headset.

None of it changes the record on paper. But a coach hired specifically to restore glory getting dragged for roasting a paying customer's hairline is, at minimum, not the redemption arc anyone drew up in August.

Week 10

FSU Wins Back-to-Back, Heads to No. 23 Wisconsin for the Game of the Week

A 31-24 win at Boston College has Florida State playing its best football of the season. Now comes a road trip to a ranked Wisconsin team in this week's marquee matchup.

By Dana Marchetti · The Renegade Report

Florida State has quietly won two straight, following a 35-14 rout of Clemson with a 31-24 road win at Boston College to move to 6-3, 4-2 in the ACC. It's the best stretch of Guardiola's second stint in Tallahassee, and it's arriving just as the program confirmed both coordinators will be dismissed at season's end regardless of how the rest of the year goes — an awkward backdrop for a team playing its best football of the year.

This week is the biggest test of that momentum: a trip to No. 23 Wisconsin, the Dispatch's game of the week. The Badgers have won their last two by a combined margin that undersells how competitive both were, and a road environment against a ranked, physical opponent is exactly the kind of game that will say whether FSU's turnaround is real.

Recruiting has held steady with the class at No. 19 nationally. Two straight wins from a coach fending off a viral moment and an outgoing staff is not the story anyone wrote in August, but it's the one being written now.
