Preseason

Guardiola Comes Home — And Brings Championship Expectations With Him

The Seminoles didn't just keep their talent this offseason. They got their old coach back, fresh off a title at USC.

By Dana Marchetti · The Renegade Report

Fall camp closed the way it usually does at Florida State: with more questions about who plays too many positions well than who doesn't play any well enough. This year there's a new wrinkle — the man running the whole operation is Pipo Guardiola, back in Tallahassee for the first time since he left for USC, a national championship ring now in his pocket.

The homecoming storyline writes itself, but the roster underneath it is what actually has this building buzzing. The starting lineup reads like a preseason All-Conference ballot.

Nobody in this building is talking about just making a bowl game. The bar is the same as it's always been in Tallahassee.

Bye Week Brings a Silver Lining: Florida State's Class Cracks the Top 40

Still 4-3, still on the bye, still stinging from the Thorne miss — but the Seminoles' 2027 class checked in at No. 35 nationally.

By Dana Marchetti · The Renegade Report

Florida State's bye week hasn't changed the record — still 4-3, still 2-2 in the ACC — but it has brought some clarity on the recruiting side. The 2027 class checked in this week at No. 35 in the national rankings.

On the field, this bye is about self-scouting more than anything else.
