Week 10

No. 7 Boston State Stays Unbeaten Past Arizona State, 35-13, Heads to Boston College

A comfortable non-conference win pushed Boston State to 9-0 and a new high of No. 7 nationally. This week's trip to Boston College is next.

By Priya Nakamura · The Liberty Ledger

Boston State handled a shorthanded Arizona State team 35-13 in a non-conference matchup, moving to 9-0 overall and climbing to a program-best No. 7 nationally. It's the highest ranking this program has held since the jump to the ACC, and it arrives during the busiest off-field stretch the program has had all year.

The biggest news had nothing to do with the scoreboard: Aramco will sponsor a full stadium renovation, increasing capacity, with the facility reopening next season as Aramco War Memorial Stadium — the latest and largest single piece of the Kingdom Holding Company-fueled buildout, and easily the biggest infrastructure story in the league this year.

Less impressive was Kanoff's performance at the podium, where he referred to his own running back, Joe Jackson, as "Joe Johnson" twice in the same answer — an apparent mix-up with the former NBA player of a similar name. Kanoff laughed it off afterward, and Jackson posted a single word in response on social media: "rude." On the trail, the class held steady at No. 13 nationally behind Cliff Truman, Mitch Britt, Fredrick Vaughns, and the recently added Azumah twins. Boston College is next, still unbeaten and still climbing.

Week 11

Unbeaten Boston State Routs City Rival Boston College, 38-14, Heads to No. 20 Wisconsin

A dominant rivalry win pushed Boston State to 10-0. Now comes the toughest road environment of the season.

By Priya Nakamura · The Liberty Ledger

Boston State took care of business against city rival Boston College, 38-14, to move to 10-0, 6-0 in the ACC — a tenth straight win to open the season and the most lopsided margin yet in the rivalry. The Colonials travel to No. 20 Wisconsin next in the Dispatch's game of the week, the toughest true road environment this roster has faced all year.

Star quarterback Avery Johnson capped the win with one of his best performances of the season, and was spotted celebrating afterward at a local Irish pub near campus — the kind of low-key postgame scene that usually doesn't make news, though it generated some local chatter this week that the Dispatch hasn't independently confirmed and isn't going to speculate further on.

Recruiting held at No. 13 nationally. An unbeaten season, a signature road test at Wisconsin on deck, and a fanbase riding as high as it's ever been — Boston State's rise shows no signs of slowing down.

Bowl Season (Peach Bowl)

Perfect and Ranked No. 2: Boston State Wins the ACC, Routs Nebraska in the Peach Bowl

An ACC title over Virginia and a playoff win over Nebraska later, Boston State is one win away from a national championship game. The staff is already being upgraded for next year.

By Priya Nakamura · The Liberty Ledger

Boston State finished the perfect regular season it was building toward all year, handling Virginia comfortably to win the ACC Championship and climbing to No. 2 nationally. The Colonials didn't slow down in the playoff opener either, beating Nebraska 14-7 in the Peach Bowl to move to 14-0 and set up a national semifinal-style date with the winner of Kentucky and Alabama's Fiesta Bowl.

The program made its next move before the confetti even settled: Luke Fickell has been hired as defensive coordinator and Will Stein as offensive coordinator, two of the more sought-after names on the coaching carousel this cycle. It's the latest signal that Kingdom Holding Company's investment isn't just funding rosters anymore — it's funding championship-level infrastructure at every level of the staff.

Two years removed from FCS football, Boston State is a win away from playing for a national championship. Whatever anyone still wants to say about how the program got here, at 14-0, the results are simply the results.
