Guardiola Walks Back Coordinator Firings, Says FSU Will Hit the Portal for a QB

A 24-10 loss at Wisconsin snapped Florida State's win streak. Bigger news came off the field: the coordinators might be sticking around after all.

By Dana Marchetti · The Renegade Report

Florida State's two-game winning streak ended at Camp Randall, 24-10, in a game that never really got away from Wisconsin's ground game. The Seminoles drop to 6-4, 4-3 in the ACC, and travel to North Carolina next looking to get back on track.

The bigger headline came at the podium, where Guardiola walked back his own earlier statement that both coordinators would be dismissed after the season, telling reporters "nothing is final" and that the staff's future would be evaluated "the right way, not in a press conference." He also confirmed the program will be looking to the transfer portal for a quarterback next season, the clearest signal yet that the position is far from settled heading into the offseason.

Recruiting held steady at No. 19 nationally. Between the walkback, the portal news, and a loss to snap the streak, it's been a busy week in Tallahassee for reasons that have very little to do with what happened on the scoreboard.
