No. 25 Florida State Closes the Season With a Gasparilla Bowl Win Over Texas, 21-13

A rocky middle stretch of the season ends on a high note. Mike Norvell joins Guardiola's staff as offensive coordinator heading into next year.

By Dana Marchetti · The Renegade Report

Florida State closed out year one of the Guardiola homecoming with a 21-13 win over Texas in the Gasparilla Bowl, finishing 9-4, 5-3 in the ACC, and cracking the final poll at No. 25. It's a solid, if unspectacular, ending to a season defined as much by off-field noise as anything that happened on the field.

The offseason is already underway: Mike Norvell has joined Guardiola's staff as offensive coordinator, a notable hire after a year in which the program walked back its own coordinator-firing decision mid-season and openly discussed hitting the transfer portal at quarterback. Between the new hire and the bowl win, there's real momentum to build on heading into year two.

Recruiting closed the cycle at No. 19 nationally. A hairline joke, a coordinator U-turn, and a bowl win later, Florida State's first season back under Guardiola is officially in the books.
