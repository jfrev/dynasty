Guardiola Under Fire After Mocking a Fan's Hairline During the Week 6 Loss to Boston State

Sideline video from Florida State's Week 6 defeat to Boston State resurfaced this week, and it has not gone well for the head coach.

By Dana Marchetti · The Renegade Report

It took three weeks for it to surface, but a clip from Florida State's Week 6 home loss to Boston State is now the biggest story surrounding the program — not the 2-2 conference record. Sideline video shows Pipo Guardiola, mid-timeout, gesturing toward a heckling fan and mouthing something about the man's hairline before turning back to his headset. The fan's friends filmed the reaction. It has not stopped circulating since.

Guardiola hasn't addressed it directly in a press setting yet, and the program's official statement so far amounts to "the coach's focus is on football." That has not satisfied a fanbase already frustrated with a 4-3 record and a five-star quarterback who picked Ole Miss over the Seminoles last week. If Odysseus needed ten years and a personal grudge from Poseidon to get home, Guardiola's return to Tallahassee is starting to feel like its own kind of long, cursed voyage.

None of it changes the record on paper. But a coach hired specifically to restore glory getting dragged for roasting a paying customer's hairline is, at minimum, not the redemption arc anyone drew up in August.
