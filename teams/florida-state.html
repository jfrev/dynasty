Week 10

FSU Wins Back-to-Back, Heads to No. 23 Wisconsin for the Game of the Week

A 31-24 win at Boston College has Florida State playing its best football of the season. Now comes a road trip to a ranked Wisconsin team in this week's marquee matchup.

By Dana Marchetti · The Renegade Report

Florida State has quietly won two straight, following a 35-14 rout of Clemson with a 31-24 road win at Boston College to move to 6-3, 4-2 in the ACC. It's the best stretch of Guardiola's second stint in Tallahassee, and it's arriving just as the program confirmed both coordinators will be dismissed at season's end regardless of how the rest of the year goes — an awkward backdrop for a team playing its best football of the year.

This week is the biggest test of that momentum: a trip to No. 23 Wisconsin, the Dispatch's game of the week. The Badgers have won their last two by a combined margin that undersells how competitive both were, and a road environment against a ranked, physical opponent is exactly the kind of game that will say whether FSU's turnaround is real.

Recruiting has held steady with the class at No. 19 nationally. Two straight wins from a coach fending off a viral moment and an outgoing staff is not the story anyone wrote in August, but it's the one being written now.
