Preseason

Kentucky's New Coach Brings a Championship Blueprint — and a Reworked Trenches-First Identity

LaSperm arrives from Syracuse with a title on his résumé and a plan to win the Big 12 the same way he won up north: in the trenches.

By Callie Broussard · The Bluegrass Blitz

There is no easy schedule in the Big 12, and Kentucky's new head coach isn't pretending otherwise. LaSperm arrives from Syracuse, where he built a national champion on the strength of a physical offensive and defensive line.

Nobody outside the building expects a run at the top of the standings in year one under a new coach. Nobody inside it is ruling it out, either.

Week 8

No. 11 Kentucky Takes Its Perfect Record on the Road to Arizona State — With a Top-10 Class Behind It

LaSperm's first season is off to a 7-0 start, and now the No. 7 recruiting class in the country.

By Callie Broussard · The Bluegrass Blitz

Seven weeks into LaSperm's first season, the copy-paste from his Syracuse blueprint is working almost exactly as advertised: control the trenches, lean on defense, let the record speak.

Week 9

No. 5 Kentucky Rolls Past Arizona State, 28-3, Takes Perfect Record to West Virginia

The toughest test of the season turned into the least dramatic result of it. Kentucky is 8-0, No. 5 in the country, and still finding a higher gear.

By Callie Broussard · The Bluegrass Blitz

So much for a coin-flip game. Kentucky went into Tempe and left with a 28-3 win over Arizona State that was never as close as the pregame hype suggested, LaSperm's trenches-first identity smothering a Sun Devils offense that had scored freely all season.

Recruiting held steady with an addition: three-star safety Donald Daniel joined the 2027 class this week, which now sits at No. 9 nationally.

Week 10

No. 2 Kentucky Blows Out Colorado 42-7, Stays Perfect Into a Bye Week

Kentucky's most lopsided win of the season pushed the Wildcats to 10-0 and No. 2 nationally. They'll enjoy the ranking during a bye before the stretch run.

By Callie Broussard · The Bluegrass Blitz

Whatever doubt lingered after a closer-than-expected win over West Virginia, Kentucky erased it in Boulder. LaSperm's team routed Colorado 42-7 to move to 10-0, 6-0 in the Big 12, and the committee rewarded the performance with a jump to No. 2 nationally — the highest ranking this program has ever held. A bye week arrives at a good time to let that sink in before the final stretch.

The program's biggest headline this week had nothing to do with the scoreboard. LaSperm reportedly left a booster function early and visibly upset after a booster referred to his girlfriend as "Mrs. LaSperm" at a drinking event. The coach hasn't commented publicly, and the program has declined to elaborate further, but multiple people in attendance described the room going quiet fast.

Recruiting held steady at No. 11 nationally behind four-star wideout Dom Chin. Between the ranking, the perfect record, and now this, Kentucky is generating headlines on and off the field in equal measure — which, for a program used to being an afterthought, is a genuinely new experience.
