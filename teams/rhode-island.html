Rhode Island's Season to Remember: The Army Upset That Changed Everything, Now the Texas Bowl vs. Florida

A missed field goal in the season finale sent shockwaves through the whole conference. For Rhode Island, it was the signature win of the program's history.

By Tomas Reyes · The Ocean State Outlook

No result this season carried more weight across the Big 12 than Rhode Island's regular-season finale upset of Army, a game the Ghost Bats won when a late Army field goal sailed wide, knocking the Black Knights out of the conference title picture entirely. For Rhode Island, it was simply the biggest win in program history, closing the regular season at 8-4, 5-3 in the Big 12, and No. 21 nationally.

Tanka Jahari's team now gets a proper reward for the season: a trip to the Texas Bowl against Florida, a genuine blue-blood name on the schedule for a program that was in the FCS-adjacent conversation not long ago. Nobody in the building is treating it as anything but the biggest stage this roster has ever played on.

Recruiting held at No. 8 nationally. Between the Army result and the Florida matchup, this is, by any measure, the best season Rhode Island football has ever had — and it isn't over yet.
