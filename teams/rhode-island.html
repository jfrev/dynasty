Back in the Rankings: No. 24 Rhode Island Returns to the Poll During Its Bye Week

A three-point road loss knocked the Ghost Bats out of the Top 25 two weeks ago. This week, without playing a snap, they're back in it at No. 24.

By Tomas Reyes · The Ocean State Outlook

If the last two weeks were Rhode Island's own detour off course, this one is the return to Ithaca — No. 24 in the country, back in the rankings during a bye week, no game required to make the case. Tanka Jahari's team fell out after a three-point loss at Houston, and the poll's committee apparently agreed with the locker room that it was a blip rather than a trend.

There's no game this week to build on the momentum, which means the story is entirely about what's building behind the scenes. The 2027 recruiting class jumped to No. 35 nationally after adding four-star linebacker Jesse Kopp and three-star kicker Mike Naane, the program's best single-week haul of the cycle.

Jahari, as always, downplayed the ranking in front of the team. But for a first-year coach who wasn't supposed to sniff the polls at all this season, getting back in after one bad week is its own kind of statement.
