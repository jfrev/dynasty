New Coach, Same Blueprint: Dirk Diggler Inherits Another Load-Bearing Offensive Line

Wisconsin's new head coach spent his career at Kent State and Auburn. In Madison, he's found a roster built exactly the way he likes to coach.

By Erik Solheim · The Camp Randall Chronicle

You could set your watch by a Wisconsin preseason storyline: a new running back nobody outside the state has heard of yet, an offensive line that returns most of a group already considered one of the best in the league.

Don't expect fireworks in August. Expect Diggler's Badgers to still be playing their brand of football in the fourth quarter of every game on the schedule.

Wisconsin Shocks No. 4 Miami, 38-21, in the Biggest Win of Dirk Diggler's Tenure

Three losses in, the Badgers needed a statement. They got the loudest one available, taking down a top-five team by 17.

By Erik Solheim · The Camp Randall Chronicle

Forget everything written about Wisconsin's .500 start through seven weeks — the Badgers just beat a top-five team by three scores. Wisconsin took down No. 4 Miami 38-21, the single biggest win of Dirk Diggler's head coaching career.

Week 9

Wisconsin's Own Odyssey: From Written Off to Back in the Conversation

Six weeks of drifting, one signature win over a top-five team, and Dirk Diggler's crew suddenly looks like a program that found its way home.

By Erik Solheim · The Camp Randall Chronicle

Every good epic needs a turning point, and Wisconsin's arrived two weeks ago in the form of a 38-21 beatdown of a top-five Miami team. The Badgers have carried that momentum into the bye-adjacent stretch of the schedule.

Recruiting-wise, the 2027 class remains modest at No. 84 nationally with four-star guard Josh Sambrailo as the lone commitment.

Week 10

No. 23 Wisconsin Cracks the Rankings for the First Time, Hosts FSU in the Game of the Week

A bye week did nothing to slow Wisconsin down in the polls. The Badgers are ranked for the first time all season and host a red-hot Florida State team.

By Erik Solheim · The Camp Randall Chronicle

Wisconsin used its bye week to do something it hadn't done all season: crack the rankings. The Badgers check in at No. 23 nationally on the strength of back-to-back wins over Miami and Pitt, and the timing couldn't be better — Florida State, winners of two straight of its own, visits Camp Randall in the Dispatch's game of the week.

The formula hasn't changed since Diggler arrived: win the line of scrimmage, run the ball until the defense caves, make the other team play from behind. It's a style built for exactly this kind of measuring-stick game against a talented but inconsistent opponent.

Recruiting added a name during the bye — four-star defensive tackle Sidney Droege — pushing the 2027 class to No. 56 nationally. A first-ever ranking and a marquee home game in the same week is new territory for this program, and nobody in Madison seems interested in treating it any other way than business as usual.
