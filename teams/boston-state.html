Unbeaten Boston State Routs City Rival Boston College, 38-14, Heads to No. 20 Wisconsin

A dominant rivalry win pushed Boston State to 10-0. Now comes the toughest road environment of the season.

By Priya Nakamura · The Liberty Ledger

Boston State took care of business against city rival Boston College, 38-14, to move to 10-0, 6-0 in the ACC — a tenth straight win to open the season and the most lopsided margin yet in the rivalry. The Colonials travel to No. 20 Wisconsin next in the Dispatch's game of the week, the toughest true road environment this roster has faced all year.

Star quarterback Avery Johnson capped the win with one of his best performances of the season, and was spotted celebrating afterward at a local Irish pub near campus — the kind of low-key postgame scene that usually doesn't make news, though it generated some local chatter this week that the Dispatch hasn't independently confirmed and isn't going to speculate further on.

Recruiting held at No. 13 nationally. An unbeaten season, a signature road test at Wisconsin on deck, and a fanbase riding as high as it's ever been — Boston State's rise shows no signs of slowing down.
