No. 16 Boston State Hosts NC State With the Program's Biggest Off-Field Story Playing Out Elsewhere

The Colonials climbed to No. 16 and stayed undefeated. Meanwhile, the biggest headline involving this coaching staff isn't even happening in Boston.

By Priya Nakamura · The Liberty Ledger

Boston State moved up four spots to No. 16 this week and hosts NC State still unbeaten at 7-0, 4-0 in the ACC — genuinely good news for a program that's spent the year being talked about as much for its bank account as its win total. On the field, Kanoff's group keeps doing exactly what it's done all season: win comfortably, recruit aggressively, repeat.

Off the field is a different story entirely, and not this program's own — see the Dispatch's National News desk for the full, still-developing account of what's happened to Kanoff's former program at Midwestern University this week. It's a lot to process for a fanbase that would rather just talk about NC State.

On the trail, the 2027 class added four-star cornerback Juan Isaac this week, though the overall class ranking slipped to No. 12 nationally, down from No. 6. Whether that dip is recruiting noise or an early ripple effect of this week's headlines elsewhere is a question worth revisiting in a few weeks.
