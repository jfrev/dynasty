Arizona State Looks to Regroup Against No. 6 Utah After a Rough Homecoming

A 28-3 loss to Kentucky was about as bad as it gets. Now the Sun Devils host the No. 6 team in the country with zero time to feel sorry for themselves.

By Nico Alvarado · The Desert Fork Report

Every odyssey has the chapter where the hero gets swept badly off course, and Arizona State found theirs at home against Kentucky: a 28-3 defeat that was never really competitive after the first quarter. It's the kind of result that raises real questions after six straight wins made this program look like one of the best stories in the country.

There's no time to sit with it. No. 6 Utah comes to Tempe this week, and Slammin Beers has kept the message simple in front of the team: one bad half of football doesn't undo six good weeks, but it does mean this week's preparation has to be sharper than any before it.

On the trail, the 2027 class took a hit of its own — three-star tight end Emanuel Guillermo decommitted this week, though the staff added three-star edge Vince Harper to soften the blow. The class now sits at No. 11 nationally, down from No. 5. Between the loss and the recruiting news, this is easily the toughest week of Beers' short head coaching career.
