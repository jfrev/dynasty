Heartbreak in the Finale: Army Missed Field Goal Sends Season Off the Rails, Misses Playoff Entirely

A missed kick in the final seconds cost Army the Big 12 Championship game and, ultimately, a playoff spot. Now the coach who planned to leave is staying, and needs somewhere to live.

By Colonel Pete Renner (Ret.) · The Long Gray Line Dispatch

This is the story of the week, and it isn't close. Army's season came apart in the final seconds of its regular-season finale against Rhode Island, a missed field goal as time expired turning what would have been a Big 12 Championship Game appearance into a stunning upset loss. Army finishes 11-2, 6-2 in the Big 12, closing at No. 16 in the country — a fine season by most standards, and an utter gut punch by the one that mattered most this week.

The miss knocked Army out of the Big 12 title game and, with it, any path to the College Football Playoff. Instead of a shot at a championship, the Black Knights will play in the Pop-Tarts Bowl to close out the year — a legitimate, entertaining bowl trip that will nonetheless feel like a consolation prize after where this season looked headed a month ago.

There's an unexpected subplot behind the scenes: Pound Da'Rock had reportedly signed only a one-year lease on his West Point-area house, intending to leave the program after this season regardless of the outcome. With Army's brass now committed to bringing him back, Da'Rock finds himself in the odd position of scrambling to find a new place to live in the same week his team is trying to process the worst loss of the year. Somehow, that might be the more solvable problem of the two.
