No. 9 Army Survives TCU, 24-21, Extends Winning Streak to Four Ahead of Houston

Army's closest game of the season was still a win. The Black Knights travel to Houston next, still unbeaten since Week 8.

By Colonel Pete Renner (Ret.) · The Long Gray Line Dispatch

Army got its toughest test of the winning streak and passed it, holding off TCU 24-21 to move to 8-1, 4-1 in the Big 12. It's the fourth straight win for a program that hasn't lost since a season-opening stretch, and the Black Knights travel to Houston next still very much alive in the national conversation at No. 9.

The option attack didn't have things as easy as it did against Air Force, but Da'Rock's group did what it's done all year when a game gets tight: ran the ball, killed the clock, and made a defense pay for every mistake late. Da'Rock himself stayed out of the headlines this week, a welcome change of pace after his AI comments made news last time out.

Recruiting held steady at No. 37 nationally behind Shaquille Lampkin, Charlie Easy, and the rest of a class still absorbing the Bulluck decommitment. Houston is next, and this program hasn't shown any signs of slowing down.
