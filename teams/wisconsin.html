Wisconsin Hosts No. 21 Pitt, Looking to Prove the Miami Win Wasn't a Fluke

The Badgers' biggest win of the season is two weeks old. A ranked Pitt team visiting Camp Randall is the chance to prove it was the start of something.

By Erik Solheim · The Camp Randall Chronicle

Every comeback story needs a second act, and Wisconsin gets its chance this week: a home date with No. 21 Pitt, the first ranked opponent to visit Camp Randall since the Miami upset. Dirk Diggler's team enters at 5-3, still three losses back of respectability by the raw record, but a completely different team by feel than the one that started the year 2-3.

The blueprint hasn't changed: win the line of scrimmage, run the ball until the defense caves, let a patient offense do the rest. It's the same approach that lost close games early and demolished a top-five opponent two weeks ago — proof, Diggler would say, that the plan was never the problem.

Recruiting had its best week of the season too: four-star safety Nate Finger, three-star linebacker Deontay Westbrook, and three-star tackle Cris Turbin all committed, pushing the 2027 class up to No. 60 nationally. Not exactly Odyssey-scale glory, but for a program that spent October doubted, it's a start.
