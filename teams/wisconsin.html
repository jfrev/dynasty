No. 20 Wisconsin Beats FSU 24-10, Hosts No. 7 Boston State in the Game of the Week

Three straight wins have Wisconsin ranked and rolling. Now comes the biggest test yet: an unbeaten Boston State team riding into Camp Randall.

By Erik Solheim · The Camp Randall Chronicle

Wisconsin's first ranked appearance of the season came with a statement attached: a 24-10 win over Florida State that was more comfortable than the score even suggests. The Badgers move to 7-3, 5-2 in the ACC, climb to No. 20 nationally, and now host No. 7 Boston State in the Dispatch's game of the week — the biggest home game Camp Randall has seen all season.

Diggler's identity hasn't changed since fall camp: win the trenches, run the ball, make the other team play from behind. It's worked three weeks running, and an unbeaten Boston State team presents the toughest version of that test yet — a program with talent and money to match, riding a nine-game winning streak of its own.

Recruiting held steady at No. 56 nationally. Three straight wins, a ranking, and the biggest home game of the year all in the same stretch is new territory for this program, and Madison sounds like it's ready for it.
