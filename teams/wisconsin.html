Wisconsin Closes a Breakthrough Season With a Sun Bowl Win, 14-3 Over Texas State

The Camp Randall magic ran out against an eventual ACC champion, but a 9-4 finish is real progress for Dirk Diggler's first season.

By Erik Solheim · The Camp Randall Chronicle

Boston State ended Wisconsin's ranked run in Madison on the way to the ACC title, but Diggler's group closed out the year the right way, grinding out a 14-3 win over Texas State in an ugly, low-scoring Sun Bowl. Wisconsin finishes 9-4, 5-3 in the ACC, a clear step forward for a program that started the year 2-3 and looked lost for a stretch in September.

The identity never wavered all season: win the trenches, run the ball, make the other team play from behind. It got Wisconsin a signature win over a top-five Miami team, a first ranking in years, and a bowl trophy to close it out. Recruiting held at No. 56 nationally, modest, but this program's calling card was never the recruiting rankings.

Nine wins in year one under a new coach, with real signature moments along the way, is a foundation most programs would take without complaint.
